dmitrii shchukin

composer / developer

studied at the robert schumann hochschule düsseldorf with professor oliver schneller, focusing on spectral analysis, algorithmic composition, computational acoustic modelling, musique mixte, and psychoacoustics. during this period worked as a research assistant developing distributed networked audio systems with bidirectional real-time data exchange, networked control, and coordinated processing across multiple computers, while software development increasingly became part of the same practice. later worked as a guest lecturer at kreativInstitut ostwestfalen-lippe in detmold, teaching electroacoustics, audiovisual technologies, networked audio systems, and software development for acoustic applications, and moved further into software engineering through qa automation and backend development. current work focuses on java-based server-side systems, databases, apis, distributed architectures, deployment, and infrastructure.
