dmitrii shchukin

composer / developer

after graduating from the saint petersburg conservatory, continued studies at the robert schumann hochschule düsseldorf with professor oliver schneller, focusing on spectral analysis, algorithmic composition, computational acoustic modelling, musique mixte, and psychoacoustics. during this period worked as a research assistant developing distributed networked audio systems with bidirectional real-time data exchange, networked control, and coordinated processing across multiple computers. later worked as a guest lecturer at kreativInstitut ostwestfalen-lippe in detmold, teaching electroacoustics, audiovisual technologies, networked audio systems, and software development for acoustic applications.

professional practice subsequently expanded into software engineering, first through qa automation and later into backend development, with a current specialization in java-based server-side systems, databases, apis, distributed architectures, deployment, and infrastructure.
