xenakis, metastasis,
and drawing sound

this is placeholder text, but the layout is real. the useful part here is seeing how a long title, an image and a few different text blocks can breathe inside the same page.

i like scores that do not merely record the result but show the thinking that produced it. a line can be a glissando, obviously, but it can also be a trajectory, a pressure, a density change, a piece of architecture pretending to be notation.

sometimes the picture becomes more memorable than the sound itself. that is probably suspicious, but also interesting. there is something satisfying about watching a musical decision become spatial before it becomes audible.

maybe the diagram is not an illustration of the music. maybe the music is one possible reading of the diagram.

anyway, this paragraph currently exists mostly so i can scroll the page and decide whether i like the margins. that is a perfectly respectable purpose for a paragraph.
