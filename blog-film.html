watched
last night

a small demo for film notes, where the image can lead and the text can sit a little differently from the other posts.

the film was slow in the useful sense of the word. not empty, just unwilling to rescue every second from silence. after ten minutes i stopped waiting for the next important thing and started noticing the ordinary ones.

there was a road, a window, a room, some repetition. probably none of that is a review. fortunately this is a blog and nobody appointed an editor.

maybe a good film can change the speed at which you look at the room after it ends.

also: the fictional film in this placeholder post does not exist. that saves us from arguing about my interpretation for at least another afternoon.
