dmitrii shchukin

projects

software, computational music, research tools and self-hosted experiments.

  • deutsch-klub crm

    internal school-management crm prototype for students, groups, teachers, schedules, payments and task workflows, built with react, typescript and supabase / postgresql.

  • deltaMetrics

    longitudinal health-metrics tracker that turns medical test results into a searchable time-series matrix with reference ranges, history charts and source documents.

  • today

    minimalist pwa for daily task planning with local accounts, task states, offline support and light / dark themes.

  • sudo

    single-screen sudoku pwa built with react, typescript and vite, with three difficulty levels, notes, undo and local persistence.

  • computational modeling of webern's op. 10

    research software in max/msp and bach that generates score material from a twelve-tone row and analytical constraints derived from webern's op. 10.

  • stockhausen studie ii reconstruction

    algorithmic max/msp reconstruction and generative extension of studie ii, modelling serial relationships between frequency, duration, intensity and form.

  • airforms reconstruction

    behavioural max/msp reconstruction of steve roden's airforms, modelling slow spectral breathing, drifting partials and incidental noise.

  • materialität am übergang

    process-based node.js + supercollider composition that sonifies a computer's cpu, memory, system load and timing conditions in real time.

  • _radiant_heliodata_sxr_0515

    data-driven max/msp sonification of nasa donki and noaa / goes solar-flare records, mapping event morphology and flux into an electronic sound field.

  • ultraviolett

    max/msp study of resonance and spectral inheritance built from reduced analyses of four saxophone multiphonics.

  • spores and the machine

    multi-state cellular automaton in max/msp with regional real-time sonification of growth, stress, decay and activity.

  • spurwerk

    explainable node.js computational model of paul celan's late poetics, built from close reading, corpus measurements and traceable interpretive claims.

  • chat_netzwerk_maxmsp

    local udp chat module for laptop-ensemble performance, designed for four networked computers without an internet dependency.

  • vaultwarden ui automation

    typescript / webdriverio end-to-end test suite for vaultwarden-compatible deployments, with docker and selenoid support.
